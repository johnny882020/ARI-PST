The site has no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking.

If measurement is enabled and you agree, we measure page views, clicks on key buttons and form submissions (never the content). No personal identifier and no IP address is stored.

Your choice is stored in one cookie named `ari_consent` (up to 180 days). It does not identify you.
