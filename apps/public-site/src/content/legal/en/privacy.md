## What we collect

On the public site we collect only what you send in the forms: name, e-mail, organization, role and message. Please do not send medical or identifying details.

## Measurement

Usage measurement (if enabled) is cookie-less, uses no personal identifier and runs only after you agree. You can change your choice at any time with "Privacy settings" in the page footer.

## Retention and deletion

Messages are passed to the office mailbox and removed from the forms system after delivery plus a short period (final value to be set in legal review).

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information. Contact details will be updated once the operator identity is settled.
