Draft. Needs legal review and an external accessibility audit before publication.

We aim for a site that everyone can use, including people with disabilities. The site is built to WCAG 2.1 level AA and the requirements of the Israeli standard (SI 5568).

The site supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, right-to-left reading, dark mode and respects the reduced-motion preference.

Found an accessibility problem? Please tell us and we will fix it.
